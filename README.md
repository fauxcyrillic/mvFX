# mvFX (work in progress)
Extremely minimal 1U FV-1 based FX module for AE Modular
* mono in/mono out
* no CV input
* 8 strange FX
* Very basic wet/dry mix
</br>

## Chosen FX (TBC)

### Starfield

A nice delay with phasing for a sort of "space-y" vibe, from the Mad Bean Pedals repo

Pot 1: Delay time\
Pot 2: Feedback\
Pot 3: Phase level

### AFX Reverse Delay

Just like it says on the tin - a backwards delay

Pot 1: Bit crush (?)\
Pot 2: Delay\
Pot 3: Feedback

### Last Known Copy

From the Steal This Pedal repo: a tape-style delay with 'damage' control

Pot 1: Tape time\
Pot 2: Repeats\
Pot 3: Damage/dulling/flutter

### Shimmer Reverb

A shimmer verb by Alex Lawrow based on the original by Mick Taylor. Amended to make output 100% wet (as originally intended; code circulating online has the dry signal inserted too)

Pot 1: Amount of treble in reverb loop\
Pot 2: Reverb time (max 10 seconds)\
Pot 3: Shimmer level

### 1 Head / 4 Reverb (Dervish)

A general-purpose reverb from the Dervish library

Pot 1: Echo time\
Pot 2: Echo feedback\
Pot 3: Damping

### 55 Gallon Saint

A metallic chorus. Source: Steal This Pedal

Pot 1: Movement rate\
Pot 2: Chorus depth\
Pot 3: Metallic feedback

### Water Rev 1

A watery chorus/tremolo from Dogman Devices

Pot 1: Tremolo depth\
Pot 2: Rate\
Pot 3: Chorus depth

### Gravity Clerk

A granular sort of pitch shifting arpeggio thing from Steal This Pedal

Pot 1: Grain size/smoothness\
Pot 2: Regenerative feedback\
Pot 3: Falling (CCW) or rising (CW) pitch motion
